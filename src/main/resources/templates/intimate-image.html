error

This form is for one thing: an intimate image of you, or of someone you are acting for, posted on Docket without consent. It is not the ordinary report route, and you do not need an account.

If you can tell us where it is precisely enough for us to find it, it is hidden the moment this form arrives — before anyone has looked at it. A person then confirms the removal or puts it back, and both you and the person who posted it are told either way.

If you cannot point at it precisely — because it is in a private conversation you cannot see — send the form anyway and say what you know. It goes to the front of the queue.

Required by law

Content is taken down on the strength of this report, before it is checked. That is a real cost borne by whoever posted it, and it is why the declarations above are required and why a false report is itself something that can be acted on.
