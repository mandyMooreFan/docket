Member conduct policy

This is the whole list. Everything not on it is not an offence. If something you did is not described below, no rule covers it, and nobody here gets to invent one afterwards.

A member may not

  1. Post or send illegal content.
  2. Impersonate a specific real person. This is the only rule about names. You can use whatever name you like, including one that is not on your passport — what you cannot do is claim to be a particular someone else.
  3. Harass a specific member.
  4. Send bulk or commercial spam.
  5. Post or send pornographic content. This applies to everyone here, not only to members under 18.
  6. Post or send content encouraging, promoting or giving instructions for suicide, self-harm, or eating disorders. This applies to everyone here too.

The last two are prohibited for every member deliberately. Some services allow this material for adults and then try to keep under-18s away from it with age checks. Docket does not allow it at all, which means there is nothing to build an age check around.

How these are enforced

Reactively, and on report. Nothing on this page is enforced by scanning what you write. One person reads reports and decides, and the response is meant to fit what happened: first the item is removed; then, if that is not enough, the specific thing that was misused is taken away — posting jobs, or replying, or messaging — for a stated period or indefinitely. Someone who misused job postings does not thereby lose their correspondence.

Beyond that there are two rungs left: an account can be made read-only, where you can still sign in and read everything; and an account can be ended.

You are told which rung, and why, and you can appeal once. If a capability was taken from you, the product says so in those words — it does not tell you to go and finish your profile as though you had never earned it.
