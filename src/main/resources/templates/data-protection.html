This form is for complaints about how Docket handles personal data — yours or someone else's. It is not the route for reporting a post or a member; that is on the safety page, and it is a different thing.

You do not need an account. Your data may have been handled here without your ever having joined, and that is exactly the case this route exists for.

We acknowledge complaints straight away — the law allows 30 days, and waiting for the thirtieth would be following the letter of it. Then we respond properly.

If you are unhappy with how we handle this, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. You can go to them first if you would rather; you do not have to come here at all.
