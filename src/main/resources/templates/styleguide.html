The design language

Serif for anything a person wrote; sans for anything the app said. Names, headlines, summaries, Post bodies, Recommendations and job titles read in Source Serif 4. Navigation, labels, timestamps, counts, buttons and form controls speak in Libre Franklin. This page exercises every token in docket.css; the spec is SPEC.md §2.

16 — supporting prose
